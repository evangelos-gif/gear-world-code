## Description
Gear World helps you find products and compare prices from many retailers across the 13 Gear World websites, with one search. Gear World sells nothing itself: when you find what you want, you buy it on the retailer's own website.

ONE SEARCH, EVERY WORLD
Type what you need, tap the microphone and say it, or take a photo with the camera, and Gear World searches every Gear World website at once. Filter the results by world and open the product you want.

SAVE PRODUCTS AND WATCH THEIR PRICE
Tap the heart on any product to keep it in your Saved list. When you open the list, Gear World checks the latest price of every saved product and tells you whether it went down or up since you saved it. Your saved products and recent searches stay only on your phone, with no account.

SHARE WITH ONE TAP
Send a product to a friend with your phone's share button. Your last searches are one tap away under the search field.

CHOOSE A WORLD
Browse each Gear World on its own: Tools & Workwear, Men, Women, Kids & Teens, Home, Garden, Technology, Car, Travel, Sport, Motorbike & Scooter, Gaming and Pets. New worlds appear in the app automatically as they launch.

BROWSE AND COMPARE BY PRICE
Open a category, sort by lowest price, highest price or newest, and load more products as you scroll.

THIS WEEK'S DEALS
See the retailers' current offers, each with its end date. A deal opens the retailer's page on a Gear World website, where you see the offer and its terms.

YOU BUY ON THE RETAILER'S WEBSITE
Each product shows the price, stock and retailer. Tap "View at" and the retailer's website opens in your browser, where you complete your purchase on the retailer's own terms. Gear World takes no payments and sells nothing itself.

SEARCH WITH A PHOTO
Seen something you like? Take a photo or pick one from your gallery, and Gear World finds similar products across every world. Your photo is only used to find products and is not saved.

11 LANGUAGES
English, Greek, German, French, Italian, Spanish, Portuguese, Dutch, Albanian, Romanian and Russian.

FREE, NO ACCOUNT NEEDED
No sign-up, no ads and no in-app purchases. You can join the Gear World newsletter if you want updates.

HOW GEAR WORLD IS FUNDED
Gear World is free to use. When you buy through a retailer link, Gear World may earn a commission from the retailer at no extra cost to you. Prices and stock come from retailers and can change, so always check the final price on the retailer's website.

## App Review notes
Gear World is the free mobile app of gearworldhub.com, the hub of the Gear World network of specialist websites that list products from retailers (tools and workwear, men, women, kids, home, garden, technology, car, travel, sport, motorbike, gaming and pets).

No account or sign-in is needed.

What the app does that a web page does not: voice search with Apple's speech recognition; photo search with the camera (the photo is made smaller and stripped of location data on the device); a Saved list kept on the device that checks the latest price of every saved product and shows whether it went down or up since it was saved; the iOS share sheet; recent searches kept on the device; and 11 languages that follow the phone's language.

How to review:
1. On the home screen, type a product (for example "backpack") and tap Search, or tap the microphone button and say it. The app asks for microphone and speech recognition permission only at that moment. Apple's speech recognition turns the speech into text; the app keeps no audio.
2. Or tap the camera button, then "Take a photo" or "Choose from gallery". The app asks for camera permission only at that moment (the gallery uses the system photo picker). The photo is made smaller on the phone (max 1024 px JPEG, without location data), sent once to gearworldhub.com to name the product, and not stored. The words found go into the search box, and the products of the best-matching world come first.
3. Results come from all Gear World sites at once and can be filtered by world.
4. Tap a world (for example Technology) to browse its categories and sort by price.
5. Open a product and tap "View at <retailer>". The retailer's website opens in Safari through an Awin affiliate link, and the purchase happens on the retailer's website. The app sells nothing itself and has no in-app purchases.
6. The language button at the top of the home screen offers 11 languages. Settings (link at the bottom of the home screen) has the legal pages (privacy, terms, affiliate disclosure, contact).
7. The home screen also shows the current season (for example Christmas or Black Friday) and this week's deals from the retailers. A deal opens its retailer page on a Gear World website in Safari; the app shows no codes itself.
8. Tap the heart on any product (or "Save" on a product screen). The heart button at the top of the home screen opens Saved. When Saved opens, the app looks every saved product up again on its Gear World website and shows, in words with an icon, whether the price went down or up since it was saved, stayed the same, or is not listed at the moment. Pull down to check again. Saved products and recent searches are stored only on the device (no account) and can be removed in Saved or cleared in Settings > On this phone.
9. On a product screen, "Share" opens the iOS share sheet with the product's page on its Gear World website.
10. Recent searches appear under the empty search field on the home screen, with "Clear".

The affiliate disclosure is shown on the home screen and on every product page. There are no ads, no tracking and no analytics. The optional newsletter sign-up sends only the email address, with explicit consent, to gearworldhub.com.
