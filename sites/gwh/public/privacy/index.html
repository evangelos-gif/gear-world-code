Privacy policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

This policy explains what personal data Gear World Hub (gearworldhub.com) and the Gear World app for Android and iPhone use, why, and your rights under UK data protection law (UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018) and, where it applies to you, the EU GDPR.

Who is responsible

The data controller is Gear World. Contact: contact@gearworldhub.com.

What we use and why

  • Search. When you search, your search words, and your delivery country if you choose one, are sent to the Gear World sites to find matching products. Searches in a language other than English may be translated into English by Cloudflare Workers AI, inside our hosting provider. We do not store your searches or link them to you. We do this to give you the results you asked for (legitimate interests).
  • Voice search. If you use the microphone button, your browser turns your speech into text with its own speech service (for example Google in Chrome or Apple in Safari). Your browser asks for permission first. We receive only the text, never the recording.
  • Photo search. If you use the camera button, your browser first makes the photo smaller and removes its hidden details, such as the location where it was taken. The photo is then sent to the Hub and looked at by Cloudflare Workers AI, inside our hosting provider, only to name the product and suggest search words. We do not store the photo or write it to logs, and Cloudflare does not keep it after answering or use it to train AI models. People in the photo are ignored. To keep photo search fair and within its daily limits, we count photo searches with an anonymous code that is made from your IP address and changes every day; the IP address itself is not stored. We do this to give you the results you asked for (legitimate interests).
  • Deal emails (newsletter). If you sign up, we keep your email address, your language, the date, where you signed up (the Hub, the app or a Gear World site) and the version of the consent text you agreed to. We use them only to send Gear World deal emails, about once a week (consent). We send the emails with MailerLite, our email service, which stores your address for us and records whether each email is opened and which links in it are clicked, so we can see which deals are useful; you agree to this when you sign up. Every email has an unsubscribe link, and you can also email us to withdraw your consent and have your address deleted. To stop automated sign-ups, we count sign-up attempts with an anonymous code that is made from your IP address and changes every day; the IP address itself is not stored. The sign-up forms of the Hub and of the Gear World sites also use Cloudflare Turnstile, which checks that a person is filling them in when the form is first used (legitimate interests).
  • Analytics and ad measurement, only if you allow it. If you select "Allow" in the cookie banner, we use Google Analytics 4 to count visits and see which parts of the Hub are used, and Google Ads (Google Ireland Limited) to see which of our ads bring visitors and which visits lead to a click to a Gear World site (consent). Google then receives details of your visit, such as the page, the click, the ad click identifier and your IP address. Google Signals and ad personalisation (remarketing) are switched off. If you select "Reject", or make no choice, Google is not loaded at all. You can change your choice at any time on the cookies page.
  • Campaign links. If you came to the Hub from one of our ads or campaign links, the campaign name and source of that link (utm_campaign, utm_source) go with your click to a Gear World site, and from there with a click to a retailer to the affiliate network (Awin, or for a few retailers CJ), so we can see which campaigns are useful. They do not identify you (legitimate interests).
  • Hosting, security and visit counts. The Hub is hosted by Cloudflare, which processes technical data such as IP addresses to deliver the site and protect it from attacks. Cloudflare Web Analytics also counts page visits and measures loading speed without cookies and without following you across other websites (legitimate interests).

The Gear World app

The Gear World app shows the same Gear World sites and products as the Hub. It has no accounts, no ads and no analytics.

  • Search. Searches in the app work like searches on the Hub (see above). The app does not store your searches.
  • Voice search. If you tap the microphone button, the app asks for permission to use the microphone (and, on iPhone, speech recognition). Your phone's own speech service, Google on Android or Apple on iPhone, turns your speech into text under Google's or Apple's privacy terms. The app uses only the text, as your search. The app does not record or keep audio. You can turn microphone access off at any time in your phone's settings.
  • Newsletter. Signing up in the app works exactly like signing up on the Hub (see above).
  • Stored on your phone. The app keeps your chosen language and a copy of the list of Gear World sites on your phone, so it starts quickly. This stays on your phone and is deleted when you uninstall the app.
  • Retailer links. When you tap "View at", the retailer's website opens in your phone's browser through the Gear World site and an affiliate network, Awin or CJ (see "When you leave the Hub" below).

Cookies

See the cookies page for the full list and to change your choice.

When you leave the Hub

The Hub links to the Gear World specialist sites, which have their own privacy policies. The Hub itself has no affiliate links; the links to retailers on those sites go through the Awin affiliate network, or for a few retailers through CJ (Commission Junction). The network and the retailer may set their own cookies to record that you came from Gear World. Their privacy policies apply on their websites: the Awin privacy policy and the CJ privacy policy.

Sharing and transfers

We do not sell personal data. Our service providers, Cloudflare, MailerLite (only if you signed up for our emails) and, only if you allowed Google cookies, Google, may process data outside the UK. Where they do, they use the safeguards required by law, such as standard contractual clauses.

How long we keep data

  • Deal emails: until you unsubscribe or withdraw your consent. The anonymous sign-up counts are deleted after 2 days.
  • Analytics: for the retention period set in our Google Analytics account, at most 14 months. Google Ads cookies: up to 90 days.
  • Searches: not stored by us.
  • Photo searches: the photo is not stored. The anonymous fair-use counts are deleted after 2 days.

Children

The Hub and the app are a product search and price-comparison service for adults. They are not aimed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Your rights

You can ask for access to, correction or deletion of your data, ask us to restrict or stop using it, object to its use, and ask for a copy. Email contact@gearworldhub.com. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk, or to the data protection authority in your country.

Changes to this policy

When we change how we use personal data, we will update this page and the date at the top.
