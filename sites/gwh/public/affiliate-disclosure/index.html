Affiliate disclosure

Last updated: 22 October 2026

Gear World Hub is free to use. The Gear World network earns money through affiliate links.

How it works

On the Gear World sites, the buttons that take you to a retailer go through the Awin affiliate network. If you then buy something, the retailer may pay Gear World a commission. You pay the same price as you would if you went to the retailer directly.

On the Hub

Most links on the Hub take you to the Gear World specialist sites. Some links, such as partner banners, are affiliate links, and Gear World may earn a commission if you buy through them.

What it does not change

The Hub search orders products by how well they match your search words, not by commission. A retailer cannot pay to change a price or a product's position in the search results.
