Affiliate disclosure

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Gear World Hub is free to use. The Gear World network earns money through affiliate links.

How it works

On the Gear World sites, the buttons that take you to a retailer go through an affiliate network: Awin, or for a few retailers CJ (Commission Junction). If you then buy something, the retailer may pay Gear World a commission. You pay the same price as you would if you went to the retailer directly.

On the Hub

The links on the Hub (search results, gift guides, deals and the world cards) take you to pages of the Gear World sites, never straight to a retailer. The Hub itself has no affiliate links and no paid placements. On a Gear World site, the "View at" button of a product then takes you to the retailer through an affiliate network: Awin, or for a few retailers CJ, as described above.

What it does not change

The Hub search orders products by how well they match your search words, not by commission. A retailer cannot pay to change a price or a product's position in the search results.
