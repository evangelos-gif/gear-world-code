Cookies

Last updated: 22 October 2026

Gear World Hub uses a small number of cookies and similar browser storage. Analytics cookies are set only if you allow them.

NameTypeWhat it doesHow long
gwh-languageBrowser storage, necessaryRemembers the language you choseUntil you clear your browser data
gwh_analytics_consentBrowser storage, necessaryRemembers whether you allowed or rejected analyticsUntil you reset it below or clear your browser data
_ga, _ga_*Cookie, Google Analytics, optionalTells visits apart so we can count them. Set only after you select "Allow analytics"Up to 2 years
__cf_bm and similarCookie, Cloudflare, necessaryProtects the site from automated abuseUp to 30 minutes

Cloudflare Web Analytics, which counts page visits, does not use cookies or browser storage.

Your analytics choice

To see or change your choice, turn on JavaScript, or clear this site's data in your browser settings.

After a reset, the Hub asks you again on your next visit. Resetting also removes the Google Analytics cookies from this site.

After you leave the Hub

When you follow a link to a Gear World site or to a retailer, those websites, and the Awin affiliate network, may set their own cookies. You can manage those in your browser settings.
